Create with Tokenized Interactive Knowledge

Tokenize Your Ideas and Research

Included with membership, subject to applicable usage guidelines and XRP Ledger transaction costs. Create private or public tokenized Ideas & Research while retaining ownership of your original work. Create and manage your assets in Creator Studio, then access your work through My Library.

Ideas & Research tokenization is separate from commercial marketplace listings and does not itself establish patents, copyrights, or legal proof of ownership.

Tokenized Interactive Knowledge

Explore Interactive Knowledge

Membership provides access to the Knowledge Library platform and available member educational resources. Explore ABA-approved content with content specific AI agents and participate in eligible Learn & Earn activities using CFC utility tokens.

Some individual books, articles, workshops, or other content may require a separate purchase. CFC is a utility token used for eligible activities in the ecosystem.

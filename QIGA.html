01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

Quantum Technologies and the Next Era of Innovation

Quantum technologies are opening new possibilities in computing, communications, sensing, scientific research, and digital infrastructure, while prompting organizations to prepare for changing security and technology requirements.

QIGA focuses on how AI, blockchain, cybersecurity, and quantum technologies intersect, including how trusted data, adaptable infrastructure, and post-quantum security planning can support broader quantum readiness.

The Quantum Innovation Global Alliance connects international expertise with applied research and development, including work with ABA’s Supply Chain, Logistics & Trade Alliance to explore secure, interoperable, and quantum-ready approaches to global trade infrastructure.

OUR OBJECTIVE

Advance quantum readiness and innovation by connecting AI, blockchain, cybersecurity, quantum research, supply-chain R&D, and global policy, standards, and ethics.

02 / STRATEGIC PRIORITIES

Connecting Research, Technology and Industry

Five priorities connect quantum readiness, technology convergence, supply-chain research and development, global talent, and practical industry innovation.

01 / QUANTUM

Quantum Computing & Emerging Applications

Explore quantum computing, communications, and sensing, including research into optimization, simulation, and complex industry challenges where quantum approaches may offer future value.

02 / SECURITY

Quantum Security & Digital Infrastructure

03 / CONVERGENCE

AI, Blockchain & Quantum Convergence

Research how AI and blockchain can strengthen data intelligence, provenance, verifiability, and adaptable digital infrastructure as part of a broader foundation for quantum readiness.

04 / TALENT

Global Research & Talent Development

Connect international researchers, students, universities, and technology specialists through interdisciplinary research, education, and knowledge exchange, including opportunities within ABA’s Global Student Network.

05 / APPLICATION

Supply Chain R&D & Industry Innovation

Advance collaborative research and development with ABA’s Supply Chain, Logistics & Trade Alliance, exploring quantum-related readiness, logistics optimization, trusted data exchange, interoperability, and potential pilots or commercialization pathways.

04 / TECHNOLOGY CONVERGENCE

Building the Foundation for Quantum Readiness

QIGA examines how AI, blockchain, cybersecurity, and post-quantum cryptography contribute different capabilities to quantum readiness. AI and blockchain can support intelligent operations, provenance, and trust, while quantum-resistant security requires appropriate cryptographic migration and system planning.

⌘QuantumComputing & security
AIBlockchainCybersecurityInfrastructure

Quantum Computing

Study emerging capabilities in simulation, optimization, and computation while assessing where quantum approaches are technically appropriate and mature enough for application.

Artificial Intelligence

Explore AI for risk analysis, anomaly detection, operational intelligence, and research support, while evaluating emerging quantum-AI approaches without assuming proven advantages.

Blockchain & Digital Assets

Explore blockchain for provenance, shared records, auditability, and verifiable data, while examining cryptographic upgrades needed for blockchain systems facing future quantum threats.

Cybersecurity

Support post-quantum cryptography planning, cryptographic agility, secure architectures, and the assessment of quantum-related risks across digital and industry infrastructure.

Emerging Infrastructure

Explore secure, interoperable infrastructure and research opportunities involving quantum communications and sensing, particularly where they relate to supply chains, logistics, and trade.

06 / EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP

Olga Mamlyga

Executive Director, Quantum Innovation Global Alliance

Olga Mamlyga is CEO and Co-Founder of Quantum Scouts, working at the intersection of quantum technologies, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and secure digital infrastructure.

Her experience includes post-quantum security, enterprise technology strategy, critical infrastructure, and communicating complex technology developments to business and industry leaders.

As Executive Director of QIGA, Olga will lead the Alliance’s strategic development and international collaboration across quantum readiness, AI, blockchain, and cybersecurity. She will coordinate quantum-focused research and development opportunities with ABA’s Supply Chain, Logistics & Trade Alliance, work with DASC on global regulatory, standards, and ethical considerations, and cultivate research, industry, and educational partnerships that can advance practical applications.

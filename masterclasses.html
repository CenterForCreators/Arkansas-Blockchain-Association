  • Unique credential ID
  • Masterclass version

COMPLETION, CLEARLY DOCUMENTED

A record designed for authenticity.

An ABA Certificate of Completion may identify the participant, ABA as issuer, the masterclass, completion requirements, and a verification reference. Where implemented, blockchain-enabled verification can help confirm that a certificate is authentic and has not been altered—without placing unnecessary personal information on a public blockchain.

THE FIRST COHORT

Become a Founding Member.

ABA’s initial cohort can help shape an evolving Arkansas technology community. The permanent Founding Member designation recognizes early participation as members build careers, pursue ideas, and stay connected to emerging technology in Arkansas.

Student members in ABA’s initial Founding Member cohort receive complimentary membership while enrolled, with early access to masterclasses as they are introduced.

Mint and Earn

Your Content goes live on the marketplace. You and your buyers earn CFC tokens with every interaction.

Ready to Mint? Here is what you need:

👤 Profile Image

Recommended: 400 × 400px. Square format looks best. JPG, PNG, or WEBP.

🖼️ Content Cover

Required: Exactly 400 × 400px. Max size: 2MB. JPG, PNG, or GIF.

Required: .docx only. If using Chapters or Sections, you must label them as Chapter 1 or Section 1 inside your document. Short articles need no labels.

📄 Content File

🛡️ Wallet Requirements

XAMAN app installed to sign your Digital Asset mint and marketplace listing transactions.

🧠 Learn & Earn

Maximum of 3 questions/activities per Chapter, Section, or Article. Labels in your Word doc must match your entries in Step 6.

🚀 Post-Approval Process

Mandatory: Once approved, complete the XAMAN signing flow from your "My Submissions" dashboard. Each edition requires its own mint signature and marketplace listing signature.

Note: Admin review takes 24-48 hours. Once approved, you will return to pay the fee and mint.

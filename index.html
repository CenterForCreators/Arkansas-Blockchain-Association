WHY ABA EXISTS

Connecting Arkansas strengths with national and global opportunity

Arkansas brings together global leaders in retail, logistics, agriculture, and enterprise, alongside strengths in education, finance, healthcare, and entrepreneurship.

From Arkansas, ABA advances blockchain, digital assets, tokenization, and emerging infrastructure through national and global collaboration.

ABA connects industry, researchers, policymakers, investors, and emerging talent to turn technology expertise into practical opportunities.

Industry×Technology×Government×Education×Capital×Communities

Our work spans enterprise blockchain, AI convergence, digital finance, workforce development, and applications across public and private sectors.

ABA is working to strengthen Arkansas’s position in the rapidly evolving digital economy while building education, strategic collaboration, partnerships, and opportunities across the national and global blockchain ecosystem.

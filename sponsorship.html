      Travel becomes strategy

      Every funded trip should return measurable value

      ABA's standard for strategic representation is simple: define why we are going before we go, execute targeted relationship development while there, and bring the intelligence and opportunities back into ABA programming afterward.

      BeforeTarget meetings, organizations, policy issues, potential speakers, partners, sponsors and member objectives identified.

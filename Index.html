WHY ABA EXISTS

Connecting Arkansas strengths with emerging opportunity.

Arkansas already has extraordinary concentrations of enterprise, logistics, retail, agriculture, food, financial services, education, entrepreneurship, healthcare, infrastructure, arts and culture, and economic-development activity.

The Arkansas Blockchain Association (ABA) is a statewide organization advancing blockchain technology, tokenization, digital assets, and emerging digital infrastructure across Arkansas.

ABA brings together business leaders, entrepreneurs, technology professionals, educators, policymakers, investors, and innovators to accelerate education, collaboration, and practical adoption of blockchain technologies.

Industry×Technology×Government×Education×Capital×Communities

Our focus includes enterprise blockchain, tokenization, AI and blockchain convergence, workforce development, financial innovation, government applications, smart cities, arts and culture, and real-world use cases across Arkansas industries.

ABA is working to strengthen Arkansas’s position in the rapidly evolving digital economy—through education, strategic collaboration, and connections to the broader national and global blockchain ecosystem.
